Cheesy Crispy Eggy Bread

Got dry bread hanging about? That's exactly what you want for the crispiest, cheesiest eggy bread ever. Done in 10 minutes and saves old bread from the bin.

Time
10 mins
Serves
2
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£0.85

Ingredients

  • 2 slices leftover / dry bread (wholemeal or whatever needs using)
  • 2 eggs
  • 5 g garlic
  • 30 g lighter mature cheddar
  • Paprika
  • Black pepper
  • Salt, optional
  • Oil spray
  • Cherry tomatoes, to serve, optional
  • Avocado, to serve, optional
  • Fresh herbs, to serve, optional

Steps

  1. Heat your frying pan on medium. Spray with oil, then throw in the garlic and let it soften for a minute.
  2. Crack the eggs into a bowl. Add black pepper and a pinch of paprika. Beat it all together — no need to overthink it.
  3. Dip your bread slices into the egg mix. Let them soak up as much as they can — old bread is perfect for this.
  4. Lay the soaked bread straight over the garlic in the pan. Pour any leftover egg mix on top — waste nothing.
  5. Grate the cheese right over the bread. Let it cook until the bottom is golden, then flip it and let the cheese hit the pan and go crispy.
  6. Cook until both sides are golden, the egg is cooked through, and the cheese is crispy. Serve hot with avocado and tomatoes if you've got them, and fresh herbs on top if you're feeling fancy.

Tips

  • Never bin dry bread — it's perfect for eggy bread, French toast, breadcrumbs or croutons. Slightly stale bread is often better than fresh for these recipes.
