Prosciutto & Mozzarella Pinsa

The ultimate fridge-raid dinner. A yellow-sticker pinsa topped with whatever's left in the fridge, the cooked veg becomes your sauce, so there's no passata, no extra pots, no faff.

Time
20 mins
Serves
2
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£4.40

Ingredients

  • 230g ready-made pinsa base
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 40g grated mature cheese
  • 125g light mozzarella
  • 40g prosciutto (4 slices)
  • Small handful fresh chives
  • Black pepper
  • Garlic granules (optional)

Steps

  1. Fry the onion, pepper and tomatoes in a pan over medium heat until soft, juicy and starting to caramelise.
  2. Put the pinsa base on a baking tray, spread the cooked veg over the top as your sauce, then sprinkle over the grated cheese.
  3. Tear the mozzarella over the top and add the prosciutto.
  4. Bake at 200°C (180°C fan) for 8-10 minutes until the cheese is melted and the edges are crispy.
  5. Scatter over loads of fresh chopped chives, slice and serve.

Tips

  • Ready-made pinsa is your shortcut to pizza night in under 20 minutes - no dough, no waiting, just top and bake.
  • At the normal pinsa price this meal would cost around £6 - the yellow-sticker version brings it down to about £4.40.
