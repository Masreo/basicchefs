15-Minute Beef & Tomato Penne

The pasta dish you'll make faster than your takeaway arrives, and it tastes way better. Lean beef, a ridiculous amount of garlic and loads of fresh tomatoes turn into the sauce, no jar, no faff, just real food in 15 minutes.

Time
20 mins
Serves
4
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£5.37

Ingredients

  • 500g penne pasta
  • 500g 5% beef mince
  • 600g baby plum tomatoes
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 30g garlic (about 6 cloves), chopped
  • 50g lighter mature cheese, grated
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Smoked paprika
  • Italian herbs
  • Chilli flakes (optional)
  • Fresh basil (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil the penne in salted water according to the packet instructions. Before draining, save a mug of the pasta water.
  2. Fry the sliced onion and garlic in a splash of oil over medium-high heat for 2-3 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the beef mince, season with salt, pepper, smoked paprika and Italian herbs, and cook until browned.
  4. Tip in the tomatoes and cook for 4-5 minutes, squashing a few with your spoon and leaving some whole - that's your sauce.
  5. Add the drained pasta to the pan with a splash of pasta water and toss until coated.
  6. Serve topped with grated cheese and fresh basil.

Tips

  • Don't bother making a separate sauce - the tomatoes, beef juices and a splash of pasta water do the job.
  • Don't be shy with the garlic - it makes the dish.
  • One pan, less washing up.
