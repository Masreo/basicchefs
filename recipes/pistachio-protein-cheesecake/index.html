Pistachio Protein Cheesecake

Creamy, rich and loaded with protein, no biscuit base, no faff, just pure pistachio flavour done properly.

Time
75 mins + 4 hrs chilling
Serves
8
Difficulty
Medium
Batch Cost
£9.50

Ingredients

  • 500g Prezydent Twarog Sernikowy 3% (or any quark/curd cheese)
  • 2 large eggs
  • 40g Chocolate Pistachio Protein powder
  • 20g cornflour
  • 60-80g erythritol, or any sweetener you like
  • A few drops of vanilla stevia (or extra vanilla bean paste)
  • 1 tsp vanilla bean paste (or seeds from 1 vanilla pod)
  • 2-3 tbsp pistachio butter, for the topping
  • 2 tbsp white chocolate spread, for the topping
  • 30-40g crushed pistachios, for the topping

Steps

  1. Blend the white cheese, eggs, protein powder, cornflour, sweetener, vanilla drops and vanilla paste until smooth and creamy.
  2. Pour the mix into a lined cake tin.
  3. Sit the tin in a bigger tray and pour hot water around it - this stops the cheesecake from cracking.
  4. Bake at 160C for 55-65 minutes. The edges should look set but the middle should still have a gentle wobble.
  5. Turn off the oven, crack the door open, and leave the cheesecake inside for 20 minutes.
  6. Let it cool, then chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours, overnight is best.
  7. Melt the pistachio butter and white chocolate spread, swirl over the cheesecake, then finish with crushed pistachios.

Tips

  • Don't wait until the centre is completely firm - remove it once the edges are set but the middle still wobbles, it finishes setting as it cools.
  • Chill overnight for the creamiest texture and cleanest slices.
