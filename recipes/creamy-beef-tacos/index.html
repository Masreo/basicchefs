Creamy Beef Tacos

Crunchy Old El Paso shells loaded with a creamy, fajita-spiced beef mince, fresh lettuce and tomato. 12 tacos, ready in about 15 minutes.

Time
15 mins
Serves
4
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£6.50

Ingredients

  • 1 pack Old El Paso Crunchy Taco Shells (12 shells)
  • 500 g 5% beef mince
  • 100 g mixed peppers
  • 50 g white onion
  • 30 g garlic
  • 5 g chopped chilli
  • 60 ml passata
  • 30 g Philadelphia
  • 100 g sweet lettuce
  • 150 g baby tomatoes
  • Fajita seasoning, to taste
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Steps

  1. Heat a frying pan over a medium-high setting. Add the onion, garlic and chilli to the pan. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the onion begins to soften.
  2. Add the peppers and saute briefly.
  3. Add the beef mince, breaking it up with a spoon, and cook until thoroughly browned.
  4. Add the passata, fajita seasoning and black pepper. Stir in the Philadelphia cream cheese until the beef mixture becomes creamy and evenly coated.
  5. Chop the lettuce and halve the baby tomatoes.
  6. For each taco shell, layer lettuce, then tomatoes, then the creamy beef. Arrange on a serving plate and serve immediately.

Tips

  • Avoid adding excess liquid to the beef mixture — 60ml passata is enough to keep the beef juicy without compromising the crispness of the taco shells.
  • Chop the lettuce and tomatoes first and keep them aside — they go in fresh at the end, not into the pan.
