Granola Clusters

I first made this to clear out the random bits in my cupboard, and now I honestly make it every single week. Trust me, once you try it, you'll get why.

Time
25 mins
Serves
10
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£4.99

Ingredients

  • 100g jumbo oats (or regular porridge oats if that's what you've got)
  • 40g coconut flakes (or desiccated coconut works too)
  • 70g chia seeds (if you don't have any, just chuck in a bit more oats instead)
  • 70g mixed seeds (pumpkin, sunflower, or honestly, whatever's lurking in your cupboard)
  • 60g cashew nuts (or swap for peanuts, walnuts, or whatever's cheapest)
  • 60g sunflower seeds
  • 50g flaked almonds (or just throw in more mixed seeds if that's easier)
  • 1 tablespoon cashew butter (or peanut butter, or whatever nut butter you've got knocking about)
  • 1 tablespoon pistachio butter (or just use more of the same nut butter, no need to be fancy)

Steps

  1. Get your oven on at 160–175°C fan. Line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Throw all the oats, coconut, seeds, and nuts into a big bowl.
  3. Add in your nut butters.
  4. Mix it all up so everything's nicely coated.
  5. Spread the lot out on your tray in a nice even layer.
  6. Bake for 12–18 minutes. Give it a stir halfway through so it toasts evenly.
  7. Take it out when your kitchen smells amazing and it looks golden.
  8. Let it cool for a few minutes – don't let it go cold yet.
  9. Scatter your chocolate pieces over the warm granola so they melt slightly. Let it cool completely so it goes nice and crunchy.
  10. Break it up and store in a jar or any airtight tub.
  11. Try not to eat it all in one go. No promises.

Tips

  • Don't wander off. Seeds and coconut can burn in seconds right at the end.
  • Looks fancy, but honestly takes five minutes to throw together. No chef skills required.
  • Spoon it over yoghurt, fruit, or ice cream, or just grab a handful straight from the jar whenever you fancy. That's how we do it.
