14-Minute Beef Tagliatelle

A whole pan for under £6 — yellow-label beef and veg doing the heavy lifting. One pan, no faff, proper dinner on the table in about 14 minutes.

Time
14 mins
Serves
4
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£5.99

Ingredients

  • 400 g tagliatelle
  • 500 g minced beef
  • 100 g onion
  • 20 g garlic
  • 100 g frozen mixed peppers
  • 100 g cherry tomatoes, optional
  • 150 ml passata
  • 50–60 g low-fat grated cheese
  • Olive oil spray
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika
  • Chilli, optional
  • Garlic powder
  • Italian herbs

Steps

  1. Put the kettle on first. Pour the boiling water into your pasta pan and get the tagliatelle cooking.
  2. Spray a large pan with a little olive oil.
  3. Throw in the onion, garlic, peppers and cherry tomatoes. Let everything soften for a couple of minutes.
  4. Add the passata.
  5. Add the mince, break it up and season with salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, Italian herbs and chilli if you want it.
  6. Cover with a lid and let the beef cook while the pasta finishes.
  7. Drain the tagliatelle and throw it straight into the beef.
  8. Mix everything together, then finish with the grated cheese and let it melt through the hot pasta.

Tips

  • Boil the kettle before you touch anything else — waiting for a huge pan of cold water to boil is where your "15-minute dinner" normally goes to die.
  • Find the yellow labels first, decide what you're eating second — that's how this stayed under £6.
