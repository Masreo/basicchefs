Lemon Meringue Chickpea Cookies

These are soft, zesty cookies that taste like lemon meringue pie, but with almost no effort. Trust me, you'll be amazed how good they are.

Time
15 mins
Serves
3
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£2.29

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g lemon meringue protein powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 tablespoon erythritol, or your preferred sweetener
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 15 g white chocolate lemon chunks
  • Zest of 1 lemon

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (160°C fan). Line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Drain and rinse chickpeas thoroughly to remove tinned taste.
  3. Add chickpeas, egg, protein powder, baking soda, sweetener, and lemon juice to blender or food processor.
  4. Blend until smooth and thick, scraping the sides as needed.
  5. Spoon mixture onto tray; shape into 8 cookies.
  6. Press chocolate lemon chunks on top of each cookie.
  7. Sprinkle lemon zest over cookies.
  8. Bake 12–15 minutes, until cookies are golden and set.
  9. Cool cookies on the tray, firming up.
  10. Enjoy your cookies.

Tips

  • Make the pistachio-chocolate version at the same time, and you get two flavours in one go. Same blender, same oven, barely any extra effort.
