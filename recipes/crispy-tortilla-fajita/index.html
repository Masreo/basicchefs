Crispy Tortilla Fajita

Fast, healthy and ADHD-friendly, zero wasted effort, and it tastes like it came from a restaurant. A full 15g of garlic for serious flavour, plus a crispy, cheesy crust straight from the pan.

Time
15 mins
Serves
2
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£2.50

Ingredients

  • 100g ground beef,
  • 90g tortilla wrap
  • 30g shredded mozzarella
  • 110g mixed bell peppers
  • 90g onion
  • 15g fresh garlic
  • 20g leek
  • 15g fajita seasoning mix

Steps

  1. Sauté the ground beef with the onion, leek, garlic, peppers and fajita seasoning until cooked through and fragrant.
  2. Lay out the tortilla, cut it in half, fill with the mixture and fold.
  3. Place a sheet of baking paper in the pan, sprinkle the mozzarella on top, then set the filled tortilla directly onto the cheese.
  4. Toast 2-3 minutes on each side until the cheese forms a golden, crunchy crust.

Tips

  • Don't skimp on the garlic - the full 15g is what makes this taste restaurant-level.
  • Make sure the pan is hot before adding the tortilla so the cheese crisps properly.
