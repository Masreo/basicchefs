Pistachio Chocolate Chickpea Cookies

If you love cookies and want a change, try these. Soft, nutty, packed with pistachio and chocolate, you'd never guess they contain chickpeas. Minimal effort, maximum satisfaction.

Time
15 mins
Serves
3
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£3.40

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 1 egg
  • 40 g pistachio chocolate protein powder
  • 30 g pistachio butter
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 tablespoon erythritol, or your preferred sweetener
  • 15 g pistachio chocolate chunks
  • 5 g ground pistachios

Steps

  1. Heat oven to 180°C (160°C fan) and line a baking tray. Add all ingredients (except chocolate chunks and ground pistachios) to a blender or food processor.
  2. Blend until smooth and thick, scraping down sides if needed. Spoon onto tray and shape into 8 cookies.
  3. Press chocolate chunks onto each cookie, then sprinkle ground pistachios. Bake for 12–15 minutes until golden and set.
  4. Leave the cookies to cool completely on the tray for at least 20 minutes. They will firm up as they cool, so avoid moving them until they're set and easy to lift.
  5. That's it. Enjoy!

Tips

  • Make these with lemon meringue chickpea cookies. Same blender, same oven, two flavours, minimal extra effort.
