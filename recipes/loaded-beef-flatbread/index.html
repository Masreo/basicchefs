Loaded Beef Flatbread

No oven needed — a crispy, cheesy loaded flatbread with a beef and veg filling, cooked entirely in a frying pan with a lid. Weirdly simple, properly satisfying.

Time
20 mins
Serves
2
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£7.50

Ingredients

  • 2 Greek-style flatbreads
  • 500 g lean beef mince (5% fat or whatever you have)
  • 150 g frozen mixed peppers
  • 80 g onion, chopped
  • 15 g garlic, chopped
  • 40 g leek, sliced
  • 300 g tomatoes (fresh, cherry or tinned)
  • 20 g Philadelphia Mexican Style (or any soft cheese with a bit of spice)
  • 50 g grated cheese
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Paprika
  • Chilli, optional
  • Garlic powder

Steps

  1. Add the onion, garlic, leek and peppers to a hot pan. Fry for 2 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  2. Tip the beef mince in, break it up with a spoon, and cook until browned all over.
  3. Add the 300g tomatoes, season properly, and cook until the mixture thickens — you don't want loads of liquid soaking your flatbread.
  4. Spread 10g Philadelphia Mexican (or soft cheese) over each flatbread. Split the beef mixture between them, then finish with 25g of low-fat cheese on each.
  5. Line your frying pan with baking paper, place one loaded flatbread on top and cover with a lid. Cook over low to medium heat until the bottom is hot and crisp, the cheese is melted, and everything is piping hot — about 5–7 minutes.
  6. Repeat with the second flatbread.

Tips

  • Keep the beef mixture on the dry side. If your tomatoes are watery, just cook it down until thick — that's the difference between a crisp flatbread and a soggy one.
  • Baking paper in the frying pan with the lid on is the trick — no oven needed, and the base still crisps up properly.
