Low-Carb Turkey Crust Pizza

No flour, no dough, just turkey mince pretending to be a pizza base. Keto-style, high protein, minimal effort, maximum satisfaction.

Time
35 mins
Serves
3
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£5.50

Ingredients

  • 450 g lean turkey mince
  • 60 g low-fat mature cheese, grated
  • Mixed dried herbs
  • Black pepper
  • 70 g onion, sliced
  • Olive oil spray
  • 50 g passata
  • Mixed herbs
  • 9 slices salami
  • 120 g light mozzarella
  • Fresh basil, optional

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 200°C and line a pizza tray with baking paper. Put the turkey mince in a bowl. Add the grated cheese, a good pinch of dried herbs, and plenty of black pepper. Mix everything by hand until well combined.
  2. Tip the turkey mix onto your lined tray. Press it out into a thin, even pizza shape. Try to keep it the same thickness all over so it cooks evenly.
  3. Bake for about 15 minutes or until the turkey base is cooked through and starting to brown. Don't add toppings yet — let the base cook first.
  4. While it bakes, spray a frying pan with olive oil. Add the sliced onion and fry until soft and just turning golden.
  5. Spread the passata over your cooked turkey base. Top with the salami slices, cooked onion, the rest of your grated cheese, and all the mozzarella. Sprinkle over more dried herbs.
  6. Bake again for another 8-10 minutes until the mozzarella is melted, bubbling, and starting to brown. Finish with fresh basil if you've got it.

Tips

  • Don't make your turkey base too thick — thin and even means more pizza, less turkey burger pretending to be something it's not.
