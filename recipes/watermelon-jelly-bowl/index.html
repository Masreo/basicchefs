Watermelon Jelly Bowl

This dessert looks like it took hours, but it only takes fruit and jelly in a watermelon shell. The sweet, colourful treat impresses guests with zero fuss.

Time
130 mins
Serves
8
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£6.50

Ingredients

  • Half a medium watermelon (about 1.5 kg)
  • 2 sachets strawberry jelly (sugar-free or regular, up to you)
  • 100 ml boiling water
  • A few drops of stevia or your favourite sweetener (optional)
  • 100 g frozen strawberries
  • 100 g raspberries
  • 100 g blueberries
