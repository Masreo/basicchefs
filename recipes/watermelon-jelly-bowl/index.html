Watermelon Jelly Bowl

This dessert looks like it took hours, but it only takes fruit and jelly in a watermelon shell. The sweet, colourful treat impresses guests with zero fuss.

Time
130 mins
Serves
8
Difficulty
Easy
Batch Cost
£6.50

Ingredients

  • Half a medium watermelon (about 1.5 kg)
  • 2 sachets strawberry jelly (sugar-free or regular, up to you)
  • 100 ml boiling water
  • A few drops of stevia or your favourite sweetener (optional)
  • 100 g frozen strawberries
  • 100 g raspberries
  • 100 g blueberries

Steps

  1. Slice the watermelon in half. Scoop out the flesh with a spoon.
  2. Save the empty shell, your show-off bowl. It always gets a wow.
  3. Set aside half the watermelon for snacking or another recipe-no waste.
  4. Blend the remaining watermelon until smooth. Aim for about 600 ml of juice.
  5. Boil the kettle. Pour 100 ml of the boiling water into a jug.
  6. Tip the jelly sachets and stir until they dissolve completely.
  7. Pour in the watermelon juice and mix thoroughly.
  8. Add sweetener if you like. Taste and adjust as needed.
  9. Throw the frozen berries directly into your watermelon bowl. Don’t defrost them.
  10. Pour the jelly mixture over the fruit.
  11. Place the watermelon bowl carefully in the fridge.
  12. Chill for about 2 hours, or until the dessert sets.
  13. Slice or scoop out generous portions. Let everyone enjoy plenty.
  14. Dig in and enjoy your creation.

Tips

  • Place the empty watermelon shell in a bowl before filling. This keeps things steady and saves you from a wobbly disaster.
