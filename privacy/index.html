Privacy & Cookies

Last updated 24 October 2026

Basic Chefs (basicchefs.com) is run by Basic Chefs. This page explains what information the site uses, why, and the choices you have. Questions or requests: basicchefss@gmail.com.

Newsletter

If you sign up, we collect the email address you type in. It is sent to Formspree, the service that runs our sign-up form, and forwarded to us. We use it only to send you new recipes. Our legal basis is your consent. We keep it until you unsubscribe. To unsubscribe or have your email deleted, email us at the address above.

Votes and ratings

When you vote on the next recipe or rate a recipe, your choice, the recipe and the rating are sent to a private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script. We do not collect your name or email with them. Your vote is not linked to you.

Analytics cookies (only if you accept)

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which recipes are popular. It sets cookies and records things like pages viewed, approximate location, device type and your IP address. It is switched off unless you click Accept on the cookie banner. Google may process this data outside the UK. You can change your mind at any time:

What stays on your device

Your saved recipes, cookbook badges, vote status and cookie choice are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are never sent to us and make the site work the way you left it. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Videos

Where a recipe includes a YouTube video, it uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode. YouTube (Google) may set cookies or collect data once you press play. See Google’s privacy policy.

Hosting

The site is delivered by hosting and network providers who may log technical details such as your IP address for security and to keep the site running.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, withdraw your consent at any time, and object to how it is used. Contact us at basicchefss@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top will change with it.
