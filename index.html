Who is Basic Chefs?

We are Basic Chefs and we started our journey at home. Life was hard, and the kitchen was our safe place, it was our escape. Those simple home meals made us the cooks we are today. We learned that good food doesn't need fancy ingredients or high skillsets  just love and practice. Basic Chefs is for everyone. No complicated steps, no fancy talk just real food, made simple and most importantly, with outstanding taste, shared with you.
